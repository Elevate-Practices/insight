Where the numbers come from

In a real Elevate dashboard, production, collections, new patients, rooms, days open and provider figures come from each office's practice software through its monthly Practice Metrics.

Profit, expenses, the balance sheet and cash come straight from QuickBooks. Reviews, calls, clicks and directions come from each office's Google Business Profile, and call scores from recorded phone calls.

Goals are Elevate's standards: $30,000 production and $25,000 collections per treatment room each month; payroll 25%, lab 6%, supplies 6%, marketing 3%, overhead 10.5% and facility 8% of collections; owner profit 38.5% or more before doctor pay.
